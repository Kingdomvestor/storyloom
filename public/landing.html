Good to know

A few things
before you begin.

What can I use as a starting point?

Pasted text or an article URL.

Which platforms and formats are supported?

Output is 4:5, 1080×1350, 5–10 slides. Labeled for LinkedIn and Instagram.

Can I edit the slides after generation?

Yes. Edit text in the editor or directly on the preview, reorder, add/remove slides, undo/redo.

What can I download?

PNGs per slide, a combined PDF, or a ZIP archive.

Does starting from a template use AI credits?

No. Template path is free and makes no AI call. Only Generate from text/link uses one credit.

Does Storyloom generate images?

No. Current output is text and layout via HTML/CSS templates. No AI illustration.
