GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
before you begin.

What is Storyloom?

Storyloom turns text, links, drafts, or ideas into editable social carousels. Start with AI generation or a template, then edit and export your slides.

What can I start with?

Paste in your own text, share an article link, bring a draft, or start with an idea. You can also choose a template and edit it yourself.

Can I edit the generated slides?

Yes. Change slide copy, reorder slides, add or remove slides, and apply saved brand details in the studio.

What can I export?

Download your carousel as PNG slides, a PDF, or a ZIP archive.

Does a template use AI credits?

No. Starting from a template makes no AI generation call. Generating from text or a link uses one credit.
